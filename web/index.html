Quick start

Three steps to a defensible model choice.

  1. Upload a CSV with a target column.
  2. Choose frameworks — or let the selector size the run to your data.
  3. Read the results: recommendation, Pareto of score vs training time, diagnostics and failures.
Frameworks run in isolated environments with a hard budget. Failed or timed-out runs are recorded as results, never silently dropped.
